FAQ

Before you start

Is Comeback medical advice?

No. Comeback organizes the plan your physical therapist, athletic trainer or doctor gave you and suggests general training. It doesn't diagnose anything or clear you to play. Your clinician's plan always comes first, and if something hurts, stop and talk to them.

I already use Comeback: Injury Recovery. What happens?

Comeback 2.0 is an update to the same app. Same account, and your recovery plan, progress and Pro subscription come with you. Train and Learn are new; turn them on if you want them.

Does it work for my sport?

Yes. You pick your sport and position during setup, and drills, plans and the coach's suggestions are built around them.

Do I have to use all three parts?

No. Each part works on its own. Turn on only what you need, and change it anytime in Profile.

Who can use Comeback?

Anyone 13 and older. Athletes under 18 can use everything except posting community stories, creating teams and invite rewards, and they start hidden on challenge leaderboards.

What does the AI see?

Only what each feature needs, and never your name or email. AI features are off until you allow them, and our AI provider doesn't train its models on your data. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what's sent.

Can my coach or teammates see my data?

Only what you switch on in a team: training counts, goal names and achievements. Your injury details, schedule, check-ins and journal are never shared.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is sold through the App Store. Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Comeback. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. Everything is deleted. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel that in Settings first.
