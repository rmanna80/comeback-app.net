HOW COMEBACK HELPS

One plan for the whole comeback

Rehab, ball work, and the gym usually live in three different places — a PT handout, a coach's memory, an app you stopped opening. Comeback puts them on one page, every day.

AI COACH

A plan drafted from where you actually are

Tell Comeback your injury, your phase, and what your PT cleared you for. The AI coach drafts today's session or the full week — nothing changes on your plan until you approve it.

  • Reads your recovery phase and recent check-ins
  • Blends rehab, technical, and gym work in one session
  • Flags when something needs a PT check before you push further
DAILY GOALS

Broken into the days you'll actually follow

A full week of rehab sets, film study, agility, and strength work, laid out day by day with reps, sets, and the why behind each one — so you know exactly what today asks of you.

  • Day-by-day goals you can check off as you go
  • Built-in cautions specific to your injury
  • Progresses automatically as your check-ins improve
COMEBACK STORIES

You're not the first to come back from this

Read how other athletes — from your own injury, and the pros who've been there — made it back. Then, once you're through it, share your own story for the next person starting where you are now.

  • Filter stories by injury, so the parallels are real
  • Featured comebacks from pro and college athletes
  • Share milestones straight from your own tracker
GETTING STARTED

Three steps to your first plan

01

Set up your comeback

Enter your injury, the date it happened, and what your PT has cleared you for so far.

02

Get today's plan

The AI coach drafts a session from your phase and recent check-ins. Review it, then approve.

03

Log, heal, repeat

Check in on how it felt. Comeback adjusts tomorrow's plan, and moves you into the next phase when you're ready.
