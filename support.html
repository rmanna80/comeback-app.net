Support

How can we help?

Questions about your account, a feature or Comeback Pro? Email us and a person will get back to you, usually within a few days.

comeback@comeback-app.net

Include what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

Your account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? We'll email you a link. Open it on your phone to set a new password, then sign in again.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it, including anything saved by Comeback: Injury Recovery 1.x. Still on version 1? Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete my account.

Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings first.

How do I download my data?

Go to Profile → Privacy & sharing → Export my data. You get a file with what we store about you.

I used Comeback: Injury Recovery. Where's my data?

Comeback 2.0 is an update to the same app. After you update, you stay signed in and your recovery plan, progress and Pro subscription come with you. If something is missing, email us from the address on your account.

Comeback Pro

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store. Open your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Comeback and cancel there. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid but don't have Pro.

Open Profile → Comeback Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple account you bought with. If that doesn't work, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using Comeback

How does the coach work?

The coach reads only what each feature needs, like your recovery phase, your limits and recent check-ins, to draft tasks or answer questions. It never diagnoses anything or clears you to play, and nothing changes in your plan until you accept it. Always follow your physical therapist, athletic trainer or doctor over anything the app suggests.

Something in a story, comment or team isn't okay.

Tap Report on the story or comment, or use Profile → Report a safety concern. A person on our team reviews every report. You can also block someone so you don't see their posts.

I found a bug.

Use Profile → Send feedback in the app, or email us.
